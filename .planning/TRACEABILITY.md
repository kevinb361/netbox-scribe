**Override safety is structural, not incidental.** `pathlib.Path` defines no `__bool__`/`__len__`, so no
`Path` instance is ever falsy — including `Path("")`, which normalizes to `PosixPath('.')`. The
`args.output or default_output` idiom therefore cannot swallow an explicitly supplied path. Verified in the
installed interpreter.
